## Interpretation and clocks

`t0` is the committed deletion request time; DB time governs new retention checks.
`DONE` below means the owner's scoped private-data work is proven complete, not
anonymous data, legal compliance, or a public-launch approval. UUIDs, relationship
facts, hashes, timestamps and pseudonymous metadata can remain personal data;
restrict them even after text/ciphertext removal. Retained exceptions must be
listed in progress, backup policy and the completion certificate.

Every row below gives actual persistence, data/references, current cleanup,
recommended action/clock/horizon, and ordering/completion evidence. All new actions
are **Proposed** under the cited D IDs. Existing retention behavior remains the
baseline until review and implementation. Proposed horizons are engineering
defaults: private social content t0+30 days, sensitive identity as soon as its
durable cancellation inventory is captured, immutable minimal metadata for the
supported recovery lineage (no automatic expiry yet), moderation per ADR-016.
There is no guaranteed completion date for unresolved cases or renewed holds.

## Reuse and remaining policy conflicts

Recommended D06: never reuse account/session/resource UUIDs. Release login email and
provider-subject uniqueness only after subject-bound proofs and historical pending
intents are invalidated/drained. Registration/login/link still requires fresh
mailbox/provider proof and creates a new UUID/profile; no recovery of old posts,
memberships, blocks, reports or tombstones. Anonymous transactions cannot be
attributed by email guess. Keep issuance HMAC windows to their normal expiry.

Reserve a former handle for30 days from t0 using a profile-owned unique reservation
(proposed migration), then release it; this is a short anti-impersonation interval,
not perpetual ownership. Reservation contains only normalized handle+expiry, is
restricted and removed when due. No old-user mapping, automatic redirect or
identity link; a newly claimed handle still denotes a new UUID. A collision at
release/registration is arbitrated by one database uniqueness protocol, not two
unchecked tables. Main may choose permanent reservation instead, retaining extra
personal data; user confirmation of product policy remains U01.

Main must review these conflicts together: strict DELETED may wait beyond365d or
indefinitely for an unresolved case/renewed hold; immutable audit metadata currently
has no finite disposal path; archived ownership and action FKs prevent physical
actor deletion; immutable reply/revision UUID references remain pseudonymous;
account-erasure parent retention must preserve other authors' descendants; strict
media absence needs a total writer bound absent from developer Silo; backups retain
restricted bytes until expiry and must reconcile on restore. None is resolved by
claiming “inaccessible = erased,” disabling a trigger, deleting another user's
content, or treating accepted Phases7–8 as a public-launch decision.
